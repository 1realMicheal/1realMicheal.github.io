// about

Background

I'm a Computer Engineering graduate from Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology (KNUST), Ghana, graduating with First Class Honors. My work sits at the intersection of computer vision, deep learning, and building AI systems that hold up under real-world constraints — from segmenting skin lesions in medical images to transcribing local-language court proceedings. I'm currently a research assistant at the Responsible AI Lab (KNUST), a remote research intern at North Dakota State University's Sustainable Mobility and Advanced Research in Transportation Lab, and a teaching assistant for Robotics & Computer Vision.

I'm applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027, with interests spanning medical imaging, vision-language models, and efficient, human-centered intelligent systems.

Selected projects

Trash Sorting Image Classification Model

Multi-class waste-sorting image classifier built with MobileNetV2 transfer learning. Ran controlled experiments on how fine-tuning, regularization, and data imbalance affect generalization, iterating based on learning-curve behavior and diagnostic metrics.

Seq2Point NILM Energy Disaggregation

CNN-based Seq2Point model for appliance-level energy disaggregation from aggregate mains data (UK-DALE dataset). Built reproducible preprocessing, windowing, and training pipelines; evaluated with MAE, RMSE, and disaggregation plots.
